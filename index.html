Biography

Hailing from a small town in India, my journey in science began with a deep curiosity for the fundamental laws of nature. I completed my early education in my hometown before pursuing higher studies, which eventually led me to The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Guided by exceptional mentors and the support of many colleagues, I earned my PhD in Physics, specializing in quantum materials and thin film growth.

Today, I am driven by a strong desire to give back to the scientific community. I aim to mentor and support the next generation of students—especially those from underserved backgrounds—who dream of building a career in science. I believe that everyone deserves access to knowledge and opportunity, and I am committed to helping aspiring scientists find their path, just as I was helped to find mine.

"Science thrives when we lift each other up. I look forward to guiding students who are passionate about exploring the unknown." — Dr. Soumya Sankar
