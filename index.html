About the Lab

⚡ Pushing boundaries at the intersection of quantum physics and intelligent systems.

Rahu Lab is a multidisciplinary research group focused on quantum computing, condensed matter physics, and the application of machine learning to complex physical problems. We believe that the future of science lies at the confluence of fundamental theory and computational intelligence.

Our work spans topological phases, quantum simulation, and AI-driven discovery — all aimed at unraveling the deepest mysteries of nature.
